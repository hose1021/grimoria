# Anti-slop audit 001, 2026-11-12

Scope: rendered Grimoria screens at 1440x900 and 390x844. Routes inspected: `/table`, `/bag`, `/seat`, `/secrets`, `/player`, `/public`, `/sheet/ru`, `/help`, `/demo`, with a dealt 8-player Trouble Brewing game.

Method: each route opened in Chromium against the dev server; screenshots at both widths; DOM measurements for overflow, contrast, tap targets, focus rings and font sizes. No interface file was changed for this audit.

Verdict: two Hard Gate failures and one untranslated control. The visual system itself is specific to the product, not a generic template.

## Findings

### HIGH

1. **R-02, em dash in user-visible copy.** 17 strings in `src/ui/i18n/locales/ru.ts`, 6 in `en.ts`, and 7 in `az.ts` use `—` as sentence punctuation. Rendered examples: the bag warning "Раздать заново — Новая игра", the update banner "Обновление отменено — сделайте экспорт…", and the help line "Смерть от Истребителя — не казнь."
   After: replace each with a period, comma or colon; keep the dash only as an empty-value glyph, or replace that glyph with a word.

2. **R-03, bag support status runs together.** `src/ui/screens/bag.tsx:1445` renders four inline children with no gap or separator. Measured text: "Нужно решение ведущегоПроверено: 21Решение ведущего: 1Не автоматизировано: 0" at both widths. The same information on `/secrets` is separated correctly with `·`.
   After: render a wrapping row with 8px gap and dot separators, matching `src/ui/screens/secrets.tsx:67`.

3. **C-4, untranslated control label.** The preset cards render `t("preview")` (`src/ui/screens/bag.tsx:1213`), but no locale defines a `preview` key, so all three languages show the lowercase English key on three buttons.
   After: add the key to all three locales, or reuse the existing `bag.previewBag` label.

### MEDIUM

4. **R-03 / R-05, mobile header wraps into ragged rows.** At 390px, `/bag` puts "Справка" alone on the second row and the RU switch alone on the third, spending about 120px before the first action. `/secrets` puts "Места | Стол" and the language switch on separate rows. `/table` wraps "Публичный экран" onto two lines in the bottom dock. The navigation set also differs per screen: the bag header has no "Секреты", the table header has ten controls.
   After: one compact header pattern at phone width (horizontal scroll or a menu), with the language switch inline and the same destination set on every host screen.

5. **R-37 / R-31, no written design direction.** The repository has no design note stating the theme reason, the dials, or the accent meaning. The audit could only infer them: operating console for one Storyteller in a dim room, ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
   After: a short design note covering the dark-only theme, the seat ring and role-token motif, and the green accent's meaning.

### LOW

6. **R-06, chip labels below comfortable size.** Bag role labels compute to 9.5px at both widths; "Гробовщик" and "Девственница" nearly fill the 64px chip, and the latter's text box measures 73px.
   After: 11px minimum and a chip width that fits the longest role name, or two-line wrapping.

7. **C-3, duplicated copy and control.** "Законный состав не значит сбалансированный." appears at the top and bottom of `/bag`; `/demo` shows "Сбросить демо" in the header and again inside the step card.
   After: keep one instance of each.

8. **R-03, language segments under the 44px target.** On `/sheet`, each of RU, EN and AZ measures 38x44px.
   After: widen the segments to at least 44px.

9. **R-31, leftover vertical space.** On `/seat` the seat ring ends around 75% of the 844px viewport; `/player` leaves a similar bare bottom.
   After: center the ring and the handoff card vertically, or place the next-step hint in the bottom quarter.

## Passes with reason

- Dark-only theme: serves a night-time, secrecy-driven table aid, not a tech aesthetic (R-21).
- Role tokens and the seat ring: content-specific identity, not decoration (R-07, R-20).
- Team color on `/sheet`: the left stripe encodes the team next to its label (R-01, R-29).
- No gradients, glass, glow, large shadows, bento grids, fake statistics, testimonials or icon sets (R-01, R-04, R-10, R-12, R-13, R-17, R-18, R-22).
- Contrast: no AA failure on any route at either width (R-25).
- Focus: 2px accent ring with 2px offset through `:focus-visible` (R-32).
- No horizontal overflow on the nine routes at 390 and 1440 (R-03).
- Every tested control has a real destination or action; dialogs use Radix (R-26, R-32).
- Empty and error states render, for example the bag error banner and the empty journal (R-27).

## Delivery gate

FAIL: R-02 (finding 1) and R-03 (finding 2). Fix those before any design pass ships.
