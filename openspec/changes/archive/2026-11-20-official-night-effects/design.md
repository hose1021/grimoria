# Design

Use the shared effect activity predicate for the Witch; remove its exemption
from source death/impairment. Retain the explicit Vigormortis exception through
the shared retained-ability predicate. Keep the existing day and living-count checks.
Do not destroy the effect on temporary impairment, because States r1039 says
the curse resumes when the Witch is healthy again.

Let the triggering Mastermind day follow the ordinary day-to-night path.
Only the end of an already active extra day resolves its special winner.
At dawn after `triggerDay`, set the existing `extraDay` flag. The dead Demon
has no step while other night abilities retain their usual queue.

Sources are the matrix-pinned States r1039, Witch r2682 and Mastermind r2993.
Old Witch code comments citing r3013 are not the pinned source.
