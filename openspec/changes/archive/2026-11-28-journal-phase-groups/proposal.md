# Group the game journal by phase

Issue: #493

The game-over chronicle is one flat list, newest first. A Storyteller reading the finished game cannot follow it as the table played it: night one, day one, night two, day two, then the result.

This change groups the chronicle under its phase in chronological order. The side panel keeps its compact recent preview. The filters keep working across groups.

Out of scope: the log content, the journal format, the rules.
