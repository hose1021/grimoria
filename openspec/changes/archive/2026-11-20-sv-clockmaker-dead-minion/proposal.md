# Proposal

Issue: #219

## Why

Clockmaker r2967: "how many steps from the Demon to its nearest Minion." The
Glossary defines "in play" as a character that exists in the current game,
"either alive or dead." The engine filtered living Minions, so a Minion who was
in play but dead was ignored.

The ordinary first night has no dead players. The case becomes reachable through
the Philosopher's borrowed Clockmaker on a later night (r2421).

## What Changes

- The Clockmaker distance uses every Minion in play, alive or dead.
- One RED → GREEN test and its evidence row.

## Out of Scope

- Other information roles that count living players; each has its own pinned
  wording.
- The Philosopher first-night borrow, already fixed separately.
