## Scope

Route `/` only: the welcome page's first viewport and its three sections. The
app bar and every other route (`/table`, `/bag`, `/seat`, `/secrets`, `/player`,
`/sheet`, `/help`, `/demo`) keep the steel console they already ship. Surface
mode: Persuade. Product truth lives in `PRODUCT.md`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The welcome page is a 1-bit print-out from the game console — the night
order set as a dot-matrix call sheet, team mixes screen-printed in dither. It
refuses the dark-SaaS hero with a rounded card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (`--color-crypt`), bone ink (`--color-text`), one
mid gray (`--color-muted`) for rules and dither, the existing blue action
(`--color-amber`) as the only saturated colour. Handjet at 700 sets headlines,
card titles and numerals: hard square pixels, Cyrillic and `ə` intact. PT Mono
carries tags and numbers; Golos sets prose. Materials are a 4 px ordered-dither
checker, 2 px team stripes, square 1 px plates with 5 px corner marks, and
zero radius.

STORY: The Storyteller reads the mechanism before the pitch — nine real first
night roles in order, three scripts with their team arithmetic — believes the
app knows the game, and starts a game with the one blue action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440: left, "ОДНА ПАРТИЯ. ВЕСЬ ГОРОД." in Handjet over a
stipple halo, the lead, the blue "Начать партию" and the demo secondary, then
one mono stat line. Right, the Trouble Brewing first-night call sheet, nine
numbered rows, printing down on load. 390: headline, actions, stat line, call
sheet below.

FORM: pinned by the person — "main page to Modern Dithered Pixel Aesthetic".
The composition is the page's own; seed: none (pinned).

FINISH: the page is checked at 1440 and 390 with a real render, and the
detector runs over the changed files.
