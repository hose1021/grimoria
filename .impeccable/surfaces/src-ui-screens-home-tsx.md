## Direction contract

THESIS: Grimoria is the last video store, and tonight's game is the only tape on the shelf. Every state is store merchandise — a tape, a spine label, a staff pick, a price sticker, a rewind notice — never a SaaS card. It refuses the dark dashboard with a sidebar and a panel grid.

OWN-WORLD: tube black (#0b0b0d); shelf (#16161b); bone print (#f2efe6); rental yellow (#ffc93c) for the one action; rent red (#ff4d3d); signal blue (#3d7bff); cult purple (#a06bff); cream labels (#f4efe4) with black typewriter. Oswald condensed caps set titles, Inter sets prose, PT Mono carries system tags. Hard 2–3 px borders, hazard stripes, barcode strips, tape spines coloured by team.

STORY: The visitor sees a store that rents out one evening's mystery, understands in one line that this is the Storyteller's console, believes it because the shelf shows the real first-night roles with one crossed victim, and starts a game with the single yellow action.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440: left, the headline "ОДНА ПАРТИЯ. ВЕСЬ ГОРОД." with the lead and the yellow "Начать партию"; right, a shelf of six tapes with real role art, team-coloured spines and one struck victim, plus the "BE KIND · REWIND" sticker. 390: the action and headline come first, the shelf below as three columns.

FORM: pinned by the person — "давай что то на основе этого, мне эти нравятся больше" with the utility-label, pixel-panel and video-store references, the video store most of all.
